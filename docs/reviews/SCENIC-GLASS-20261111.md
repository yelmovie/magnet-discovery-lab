# Scenic glass UI

Buttons and guidance surfaces use translucent light fills, subtle borders and soft shadows. Text and learning objects are not faded. Reading dialogs retain stronger light backing. Focus and state colors remain distinct.

264 tests; 225 responsive state checks across 5 viewports; small portrait ring controls, footer buttons, overview villain positions repaired. Final ring 6 states retested. Natural flow completed activities 1–10, final notebook, ending and certificate, touch checks at 6 sizes. Small screens may scroll in reports and modal reading content.

Real hardware tablets and all browser/OS combinations are not tested. State-injected layout checks are distinct from natural play.
